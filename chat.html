👋 Welcome to N2 Mesh

This chat is 100% peer-to-peer. Your messages travel directly between browsers over WebRTC data channels — there is no chat server, no database, and no account. Peers discover each other the way messengers do: they announce their presence on a public signaling topic and connect straight to each other.

  1. Pick a nickname below.
  2. Open this page in another browser / tab, or share the room link.
  3. When peers connect, messages flow straight between you.

Works on static hosting (this is GitHub Pages). A public MQTT broker is used only for signaling and as a fallback on restrictive networks — your messages go directly peer-to-peer whenever possible.
